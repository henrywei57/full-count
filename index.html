BASERUNNING

E / RB
Send every runner one more base
Q / LB
Take a base back (stop at the next bag, press again to go back)
Click a runner
Pick him (click him on the field, his tag, his dot on the mini diamond, or his row in the runner panel). E / Q then run just him. Click him again for everyone.
Hold 1–4 + E/Q
Also runs just the runner on or heading to that base. Arrows or the D-pad work too.
E before the pitch
Steal: the runner goes on the pitcher's first move (hold 1–3 for one runner, Q calls it off). RB / LB on a gamepad.
Runner panel
Click ◀ / ▶ next to any runner. Each runner shows where he's headed.
